Make the golf
a duel.

Nine champions, eighteen holes, thirty-six abilities. The next step is to stop golf from being a waiting game: every shot your rival takes should be something you can answer, and every hole should feel like it belongs to someone.

None of this is in a build yet. It is the plan for the next stretch of work. Names, numbers and pictures of screens are sketches, and anything here can change after a test.

01

The duel

Golf you play against someone

Right now most abilities are used on your own turn. The duel changes that: your rival's swing becomes your move too.

I

Reactions

One ability per champion, played during an opponent's swing. A gust, a heckle, a ward.

Planned
II

Break the realm

Every champion has two home holes. There they get a small perk you can see. Beat them on their own ground and you break the realm for a bonus.

Planned
III

Match play and Bounties

Win holes, not totals. Plus a prize round where a tied hole's prize carries over to the next. Both optional.

Optional mode
02

Mechanics and feel

Skill you can learn

Fewer guesses, more reads. The ball should do what a good player expects, and the ground under it should matter.

Shot shaping

Draw, fade or punch, chosen before you swing. Wind and slope reward the right one. It builds on the finer aiming already in.

Planned

A proper putting read

A coloured putting line, plus a short pulse that shows the green's contours. Reading becomes a skill, not a guess.

Planned

Every realm's ground plays its own way. Learn it and you can use it.
