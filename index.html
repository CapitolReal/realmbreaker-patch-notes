Dev Update
Dev update  ·  24 November 2026  ·  Built since Test Build 18

Eighteen holes.
Four abilities each.

Since Test Build 18 the course has grown to a full eighteen, every champion has a kit of four abilities, the galleries have come alive, and the game has a new entrance.

Not in a download yet. Everything on this page is in the development build and will ship in the next test build. The download is still Test Build 18.

How frame rates were measured: on the development build at 1640×920, with the frame rate uncapped. The download is capped at 60 FPS, and none of these numbers has been measured in a download build yet.

01

The nine kits

Four abilities for every champion

The shared Tome and the champion spellbooks are gone. In their place every champion has four abilities of their own.

Realm Power

Charges as you play, now 25% slower than in Test Build 18. Fire it with F at a full meter.

Signature

Three uses a round, on R. No charging.

Two abilities

Three uses each a round, on E and B. All eighteen are new since Test Build 18.

Champion portraits are art from the opening cinematic.

02

Abilities in play

In-game, development build

The big ones stop the round for a few seconds and take the camera. All of these were shot on The Two Trees.

03

Three new holes, two rebuilt

The course is a full eighteen

Every champion now has two home holes. Each new hole started as a concept painting, and its models were made from that painting.

Also on Throne of Ash (hole 9): the Colossus has a new rig. He stalks in, pounds the green, then sits the throne in front of the castle and heckles you for the rest of the hole. His lines are subtitles for now; the voice is not recorded.

04

A new entrance

Title screen and champion select

The logo was redrawn at four times the resolution, with the dark haze round its letters gone. The title also has mist over the water, shafts of light, and drifting motes.

05

Patch notes

Everything since Test Build 18
New

Added

8 additions
  • Three new holes: Halls of the Great Forge (16), The Giant's Feast (17) and The World Turtle (18). A full round is now eighteen holes.
  • Eighteen new abilities, two for every champion, on E and B.
  • Pit Fight, Grukka's new Signature: a ten-second brawl in third person. The loser takes a penalty stroke.
  • A living gallery. Thirteen kinds of rigged spectators stand round every tee and green. Each has their own stance, fidgets and cheers, and they turn to watch the ball.
  • Lobby champion select. Join and Host now go straight to a lobby where everyone picks on one screen and sees each other's cursor move, like a fighting game's select screen.
  • Host game setup in the lobby: course length, Caddie Pip open or secret, mini-games, and a shot clock (off, 30, 60 or 90 seconds).
  • A club set for every champion: 27 clubs sized to each champion's hands and theme, and a staff for the Archmage.
  • Title music and reveal: a new title theme, and a camera move out of the opening cinematic timed to it.
Changed

Reworked

11 changes
  • Every champion has exactly four abilities: a Realm Power, a Signature and two more.
  • The Tome and the champion spellbooks are removed.
  • Signatures and abilities have three uses a round.
  • Realm Powers charge 25% slower.
  • Malgrath: Hellgate replaces Hellfire Drive.
  • Archmage: Seer's Sight is removed. Broken Mirrors is his Realm Power, fired at a full charge.
  • Vale of Falling Waters and The Colosseum of Kings are rebuilt from scratch.
  • All 98 synthesised sounds are replaced by recordings. The harsh chime on Crystal Mere is gone; its sound is now a quiet ambient bed.
  • Interface pass: one button style, one panel style, text centred in its boxes, and a fact shown once instead of three times.
  • Every hole got an atmosphere pass against its concept painting, plus detail textures on rock and mountains.
  • The sky-whale swims. It has a spine now and no longer holds a curl.
Fixes

Fixed

5 fixes
  • Hall of the Fallen runs at 114 FPS, up from 63. Each flying creature's shadow was claiming 400 metres of shadow map every frame. Stormcrown Aerie, Eagle's Tarn and the Vale gained from the same fix.
  • Zig-zag edges are smooth: island rims and shorelines no longer saw-tooth.
  • Models sit level on slopes instead of hanging over them.
  • Shorter wait between holes: champion rigs stay loaded from one hole to the next.
  • Text in boxes sat low and off-centre across the menus. It is centred now.
Under the hood

Testing

4 checks
  • Every ability has been played by computer golfers in automated rounds across all eighteen holes, with no errors or crashes.
  • Two machines: rounds with abilities in play, where every shot's end position is compared between host and client. All matched.
  • Nineteen menu and mode tests pass: menus, the four mini-games, the brawl, rematch, the Colossus, and map cycling.
  • Hall of the Fallen was started 30 times after the shadow fix. The old driver hang did not return.

What to watch for in the next build

Nothing here is in a download yet

All of it was built and measured on the development build. The download build caps at 60 FPS and has not been made or timed with these changes.

Two abilities online

Hellgate and Crater Cup have been played in single-machine rounds only. Every other new ability has been played with a host and a joining client.

Title music level

The new title theme was set to the old loop's level by meter, not by ear. If it is too quiet or too loud, say so.

The Vale at dusk

Hole 7 is set at dusk. The fairways may be too dark to read. Tell us if you lose the ball.

Elderwood Gate hang

A rare graphics-driver hang in the first seconds of hole 1 is still open on the development build. It did not occur in this round of testing.

Balance

The new abilities' numbers are first picks. If one is far too strong or never worth pressing, name it.
