Champion skins

Two more looks for every champion

Since Test Build 20

Each champion has two new outfits. They are new models, not recolours, and each keeps to its champion's theme. You earn them by playing that champion.

Five birdies or better with that champion, counted across any rounds.

Third look

Kept between builds

Progress is saved outside the game folder, so a new test build does not wipe what you have earned.

And the rest of Test Build 20

Hole 11 was rebuilt as the Archmage's home, a pocket of space outside the world. The dragons and the giant eagles were made again so that they fly properly, and two older holes were filled out.

About the pictures. Every picture on this page was taken in the 25 November development build. The ones in this section show Test Build 20's content as it looks today.

04

Abilities, smoothed

A review of all 36, and two ideas from older golf games

Since Test Build 20

Every ability was filmed in play on one hole and the film was read frame by frame. Nine kinds of fault came out of it. The changed abilities were filmed again, except the four listed under Not yet verified.

  • Graded approach calls (new). A shot that finds the green is called by how close it finishes: Stone Dead, Tight! or On the Green, with the feet left to the pin.
  • Banners no longer stack. An ability's title drew over the drive distance, and the shot count drew over the Warp Stone's dice card.
  • The camera no longer ends up inside a tree. Trees and bushes thin away as the camera nears them.
  • Arcane Blink could drop the Archmage under a pine in deep rough. It now looks for fairway near its spot, and never puts him nearer the pin than before.
  • Spire of Glass sinks as soon as he has struck, not in front of the next player's camera.
  • Wyrm of the Pit: the camera stands further back and the whole dragon is in frame.
  • Hellforged Driver and Spawn of Malgrath: the ball grows into a meteor, and the follow camera now stays outside it.
  • Haunted Green: the ghosts keep clear of the camera on a short putt.
  • Glacier Run lays pale, ragged ice in place of a hard white slab. The meteor's scorch has round sparks and glowing cracks in place of bright squares.
05

Patch notes

Everything since the 24 November update

In Test Build 20 Packaged on 24 November

New

Added

5 additions
  • The Starfall Isles (hole 11) are the Archmage's home. The Earth is gone from the sky. In its place: the Great Portal, his tower, three libraries, two orreries and an apprentice.
  • New dragons, modelled in flight and rigged: fire, frost, astral and gold.
  • New giant eagles on Stormcrown Aerie, with standing, take-off, flying, gliding and hovering motions.
  • A celebration for each champion when their ball drops.
  • Flags wave. The pin's flag turns down the game's wind and moves with its strength. The banners on the Aerie's nests whip in a wind that never drops.
Changed

Reworked

9 changes
  • Gloomgate Vale (hole 4) is laid out again: a winding fairway over four rises, a cut across it, and a two-tier green. Its water is now sludge.
  • Dragon's Spine (hole 2): the volcano ring and the mesas stand at the edge of the ground, and the gap between is filled with spires, lit mushrooms, ribs and lava light.
  • Lobby line-up: the champions stand where the camera can see all of them, on every hole. Small things in the way are hidden while the lobby is up.
  • Lobby player icons are lit and captured again. They had looked half-drawn, then washed out.
  • Starshard Arrow scatters wider: the five pieces fall within 13% of the carry (12 to 30 yards), up from 7.5%.
  • The swing meter runs 8% slower.
  • The putting line draws about 70% of the way from the ball to the hole.
  • Menu music is louder and plays through every screen before a match.
  • The logo is the original art with its edges cleaned and its smudges removed. On the title it sits lower and the buttons sit higher.
Fixes

Fixed

5 fixes
  • The "wall" on the far horizon. On every hole the cloud was smeared straight down in streaks where sky met ground. The sky now curves over a round world.
  • Eagle nests hung in the air on Stormcrown Aerie. They stand on the ground.
  • The Vale's temple: its stair and fountain overhung empty air. There is ground under them now.
  • Clouds raced on the Vale, Stormcrown Aerie and Hall of the Fallen. They drift slower.
  • Small interface faults: "1St Shot" reads "1st Shot", the score table no longer stretches across the screen, and the pin marker is drawn as one clean shape.

Since Test Build 20 In the development build

New

Added

5 additions
  • Eighteen champion skins, two for each champion, with a row on the champion card and over your lobby plate to choose one. A message tells you when you earn one.
  • Landing camera, with a switch under Settings, Gameplay.
  • Graded approach calls: Stone Dead, Tight! and On the Green, with the distance left in feet.
  • A second menu song. The two take turns: when one ends the other starts.
  • Eight coast mountains round the World Turtle.
Changed

Reworked

8 changes
  • Halls of the Great Forge: a far larger hall, taller hammer gantries, and a corner map fitted to the course.
  • The World Turtle: the fairway cut into his shell, his head turned back, a faster swim, an older-looking shell.
  • The Giant's Feast: a winding table runner with folds in the cloth.
  • The crowd reacts less often after shots and abilities. In a test round on the Great Forge, 5 of 14 reactions were let through.
  • Seraphine's swords circle her, centred and clear of her body.
  • Computer golfers plan with what they have armed. They allow for a power boost, lay up after a lost ball, and Malgrath keeps Spawn of Malgrath for holes long enough to want it.
  • Aurora Fjord (hole 15): the green was a dark patch at night. It has a soft pool of aurora light.
  • The Starfall Isles: an old ring-shaped landmark was showing as a black cut-out in front of the Great Portal. It is removed.
Fixes

Fixed

6 fixes
  • The computer golfer hit out of bounds five times running on The Colosseum of Kings and took a 10. It was hitting harder after every lost ball. It now makes par there.
  • Banners drawn on top of each other when an ability, a drive distance and a dice card came up together.
  • Cameras inside trees, boulders and the gallery on several abilities and on the landing camera.
  • A meteor ball swallowed the follow camera for a frame.
  • Ghosts across the lens on a short putt under Haunted Green.
  • "Her own ball" in the message when the Archmage plays a plundered Starshard Arrow. It says "his".
Under the hood

Testing

4 checks
  • All 36 abilities were filmed in play against a computer rival. The changed ones were filmed again after their fixes, except the four listed under Not yet verified.
  • Holes 9 to 18 were each played start to finish by computer golfers in the development build. Every hole completed, with no hang.
  • Skins: an eighteen-hole round was run against a scratch save file to check both unlock rules. No test wrote to a player's own save.
  • Menu songs: 13 hand-overs in a row in a shortened run, the two songs in turn each time.

Not yet verified

What to watch for

Test Build 20 had no full play test

Its title screen and six tee views were checked and nothing crashed. The automated round was started three times and stopped each time, so it never finished on the final build.

Nothing since is packaged

Sections 01, 02 and 04 are in the development build only. They have not been built into a download or timed.

Four ability changes not seen

The Pit Fight camera held inside the stakes, Stormtalon's camera kept above ground, the Cyclone's funnel, and the landing camera's rule for deep rough were changed but did not come up in a test clip.

Skins online

A joining player's skin shows on the host's screen. Their birdie count was followed over one hole only, and nobody has yet earned a skin in a real round.

Menu songs, by ear

The hand-over between the two songs was tested in a shortened run and by meter. Nobody has listened to a whole song end and the next begin.

First picks

The skin names, the five-birdie rule and the abilities' numbers are first picks. If a skin is too easy or too hard to earn, or an ability is never worth pressing, say so.

Two abilities online

Hellgate and Crater Cup have still been played on one machine only.

Elderwood Gate hang

A rare graphics-driver hang in the first seconds of hole 1 is still open on the development build.

The Colossus has no voice

His heckles on Throne of Ash are still subtitles. The lines are not recorded.
