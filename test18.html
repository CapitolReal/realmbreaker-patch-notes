02

Patch notes

Everything that changed
New

Opening cinematic

1 addition
  • The game opens with a 70-second trailer, then goes to the title screen. It plays on every launch; any key or click skips it.
Performance

A faster round

7 changes, no visual differences
  • Average frame rate up from 71 to 93 FPS across all fifteen holes.
  • Game-thread time per frame down from 12.5 ms to 5.6 ms. The HUD was the main cost. It now remembers its text layout instead of measuring every letter every frame.
  • Slow frames cut from 337 to 60. These are frames that took longer than 33 ms over the benchmark round.
  • The stutter when changing holes is gone. The HUD was rebuilding its text cache at every hole.
  • Terrain is drawn as one merged mesh, which also clears a shadow-system overflow warning.
  • Sounds preload in the background instead of on first use.
  • In the download build, hole 1 holds the 60 FPS cap.
Fixes

Bugs and crashes

8 fixes
  • Hall of the Fallen (hole 14): graphics-driver hang fixed. It struck in the first seconds at the tee, about one start in seven. Before: 17 hangs in 127 starts. After: none in 90.
  • Aurora Fjord is now a par 4. It was a par 5.
  • Lobby: mouse hover and clicks now update the screen straight away.
  • Multiplayer: each hole's state resets when the hole starts, not when a player joins.
  • Multiplayer: a player who leaves is cleared after a 10-second grace period, and the host can finish the round alone.
  • Multiplayer: fixed the check that decides which golfer is yours when players are on separate machines.
  • Spin steering: fixed a divide-by-zero.
  • Mini-game: fixed a wave that could fail to end.
Under the hood

Testing and tools

2 additions
  • Five new automated test suites: every hole rendered and played, a full headless round, menus and modes, host with a joining client, and a client dropping mid-hole.
  • Benchmark switches: -rbgbench for frame statistics, -rbgbenchfile to write them to a file, and -rbgnohud to run without the HUD.
